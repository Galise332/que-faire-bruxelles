On Chaussée de Louvain in Schaerbeek, Groseille has made a simple choice: no endless menu, just a few well-thought-out plates that change every month. Chef Camille Cosnefroy builds her menu around the season and whatever the local producers bring. So you can come back the following month and find something new.

## What's on the plate

The house describes its food as market cooking, tasty and local, served with natural wines. The menu is renewed every month and the restaurant's website gives it its own page: worth a look before you come, since it depends on what the land and the producers have to offer.

Dishes can be shared, or not: it's up to you. On Friday lunchtime, the lunch follows the evening menu, adapted to the middle of the day.

Once a month, on a Sunday, Groseille breaks from its routine: barbecue, brunch, ramen, with a different format each time. The next date is announced on the events page of the website and on Instagram (@groseille_restaurant).

## Who it's for

For people who like to eat with the seasons without any fuss, in a bright and welcoming dining room. For a group of friends who want to taste a bit of everything and share, and for a Friday lunch. The restaurant also describes itself as ideal for families. The dining room is accessible to people with reduced mobility, and pets are welcome.
