A few steps from Place Flagey, on Rue de Vergnies, Le Marni has spent 25 years programming a bit of everything that belongs on a stage: jazz, theatre, dance, contemporary circus, exhibitions and children's shows. The venue calls itself "human-sized", and that is exactly how it feels: a hall where you see the artists up close, and a bar where you can bump into them after the show.

## What to do there

Jazz is the common thread of the 2026-2027 season, with regular concerts: Master Clash around Wayne Shorter on 24 November, the Pascal Mohy - Ben Sluijs Quartet on 3 December, the Adèle Viret Quartet on 8 December. Alongside, contemporary theatre, dance and circus fill the main hall, and exhibitions round out the line-up.

Improv has its place too: the Ligue d'improvisation professionnelle (LIP) plays its improv matches here, with two teams, three referees, live music and an audience that votes (12 to 14 February 2027). For 6 to 12-year-olds, Impro Kids (20 January 2027) turns children's drawings into stories acted out on stage.

Le Marni also hosts artists in residence: the "Intra Muros" page shows the projects being created in the studio and the LABO.

The bar opens one hour before the performance and stays open afterwards. On concert nights, a small dish is served from 7 to 8 pm (book online up to four days ahead); before and after the children's shows, it's homemade crêpes.

## Who it's for

For jazz lovers who prefer an intimate hall to a big stage, for anyone curious about contemporary theatre and dance, and for families: Le Marni even hosts birthday parties around a kids' show, with a decorated table and an afternoon snack.

## Practical info

- **Booking**: online 24/7 on the website, on 02 639 09 82 or at reservation@theatremarni.com; the box office opens one hour before the performance
