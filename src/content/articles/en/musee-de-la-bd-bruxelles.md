You come for the comics, and you stay for the building too. The Musée de la Bande Dessinée moved in 1989 into the former Magasins Waucquez, a large fabric store designed by Victor Horta and opened in 1906. Glass roof, wrought-iron staircases, light pouring down from the ceiling: the setting alone is worth the trip. And across 4,200 m², it tells the whole story of European comics, from their origins to today's graphic novels.

## What to do there

The permanent route is split into five exhibitions. "L'Invention de la Bande Dessinée" goes back to the beginnings of the genre and asks how to define it. "Un siècle de BD belge" tells how Belgium became the leading comics country in the mid-20th century. "L'Art de la BD" shows the creative process, from the blank page to the full range of genres. "Le réveil de Little Nemo" brings the famous little dreamer back to life through the pen of Philippe de Pierpont, and "Horta et les Magasins Waucquez" tells the story of the building itself.

On the third floor, the major temporary exhibitions change regularly. Until 17 May 2027, "Mangatopia" celebrates 30 years of the publisher Kana and the rise of manga in French. The museum also hosts meetings with authors, guided tours and, twice a month on Sunday afternoons, sketching sessions led by a comics artist.

## Who it's for

Families first: children under 6 get in free, and comic book heroes speak to all ages. But also Art Nouveau lovers, manga fans, and anyone who wants to understand why Brussels has comic strip murals on every street corner. Allow a good half-day if you read everything.

## Practical information

- **Opening hours**: Tuesday to Sunday, 10am to 6pm (last admission at 5pm). Closed on Mondays, 25 December and 1 January.
- **Prices**: €14 for adults, €11 for over-65s and 12-25s, €6 for 6-11s, free for under-6s. Free with the museumPASSmusées and the Brussels Card.
- **Booking**: not required for individual visitors.
- **Getting there**: a 10-15 minute walk from Brussels-Central or Brussels-North station. Interparking Passage 44 car park 150 m away.
- **Contact**: visit@cbbd.be
