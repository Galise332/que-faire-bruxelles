A museum visit with kids can go wrong fast. These three hold their attention: you look, you listen and you learn without noticing. All three are closed on Mondays, so keep that in mind before you head out.

## 1. The Museum of Natural Sciences

**Rue Vautier 29, 1000 Bruxelles** · [naturalsciences.be](https://www.naturalsciences.be/fr)

The star is the Dinosaur Gallery: over 3,000 m² and dozens of specimens, the largest hall in Europe devoted entirely to dinosaurs. This is where you'll meet the Bernissart iguanodons, discovered in Belgium. The rest of the museum follows with Living Planet and the Gallery of Humankind. Closed on Mondays; last entry in the late afternoon, earlier on weekdays than at weekends. Full opening hours and tickets on the website.

## 2. The Comics Art Museum

**Rue des Sables 20, 1000 Bruxelles** · [comicscenter.net](https://www.comicscenter.net/fr)

The whole story of Belgian comics, a few minutes from the Grand-Place. Kids recognise the heroes they read, parents find out how a comic page is made. This autumn, the Mangatopia exhibition also speaks to manga fans. Open Tuesday to Sunday from 10am to 6pm, last tickets sold at 5pm. A 10-15 minute walk from Brussels Central station.

## 3. The MIM, Musical Instruments Museum

**Montagne de la Cour 2, 1000 Bruxelles** · [mim.be](https://www.mim.be/fr)

A museum where you listen as much as you look: the multimedia guide is included in the ticket price, and the collections range from the most familiar instruments to the most unusual. Open every day except Monday, last entry at 4:15pm, so go in the morning. Tickets on site or online, by time slot. The museum is fully accessible to people with reduced mobility.
