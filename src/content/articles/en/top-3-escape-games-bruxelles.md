One hour, one team, one door to open: an escape game is still one of the easiest outings to plan when it rains in Brussels. You hunt for clues, crack puzzles and work together to get out before the clock runs down. Here are three addresses in the city centre, all within walking distance of each other, each with a very different world.

## 1. Escape Prod

**Rue de l'Étuve 69, 1000 Bruxelles** · [escapeprod.com](https://www.escapeprod.com/fr/)

A stone's throw from Manneken Pis, Escape Prod offers five 60-minute rooms for teams of 2 to 6 players, from €22 per person. The themes will appeal to comic fans: you play the Daltons breaking out of Lucky Luke's cell, or investigate alongside Blacksad. The other rooms send you to rob a museum while dodging lasers, save a spaceship for NASA or find your friends in the colourful world of Brick World. Children play accompanied, from age 8 depending on the room. To keep going outdoors, the Mystery Bag treasure hunts show you the city in 1h30, from €16 per person. Getting there: metro Gare Centrale, or trams and buses to the Bourse stop.

## 2. Let Me Out

**Place de la Liberté 3, 1000 Bruxelles** · [letmeout.be](https://letmeout.be/)

Four escape rooms in the heart of Brussels for groups of 2 to 5: Wonderland, a prison break, the mystery of room no. 4 at the Blue Hotel, and Escape Bullying, where you help Sacha stand up to bullying at school. Prices are per group: €80 for two, €100 for three, €110 for four and €125 for five; Escape Bullying costs €70 whatever the number of players. Every Thursday, a team that escapes without any help gets 10% off, and student groups also get 10% off with their student card and the code "studentLMO".

## 3. Enygma

**Boulevard de l'Empereur 27, 1000 Bruxelles** · [enygma.be](https://enygma.be/)

In the historic centre, Enygma goes all in on immersive, theatrical sets. In Nautilus, you must recover a prototype of Captain Nemo's submarine; three identical rooms let up to 18 people play at the same time. The School of Wizardry drops players into a school of magic for 70 minutes of play. Games run in French, Dutch or English, from €25 per person (cheaper per player the bigger the team). Allow about 90 minutes on site including the briefing. Children are welcome with an adult. Open every day from 10am to 11pm.
