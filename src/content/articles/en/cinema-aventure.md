You need to know where to go in: Cinéma Aventure is inside the Galerie du Centre, right in the heart of Brussels, with access via number 57 on rue des Fripiers. Its three screens reopened in 2010 and have since championed alternative cinema, attentive to social realities, with a programme largely off the beaten track. In Belgium, Aventure presents itself as the cinema for documentaries, and as a place where the team enjoys talking about films with its audience.

## What to do there

You can see Belgian, European and international films, chosen for their quality and originality, along with some more classic titles. The three screens have 107, 75 and 50 seats. They are air-conditioned, and the cinema says they are fitted with the latest image and sound technology.

What really sets the place apart is what happens around the screening. The cinema sees itself as a space for exchange, open to audiences as well as associations and community organisations. It announces an event every day of the year: film club, festival, film and debate, film concert, retrospective, short film programme, masterclass or preview. So it is worth checking the programme before you come.

## Who it's for

Documentary lovers first of all, and anyone who likes to keep talking after the credits. Also curious viewers looking for something other than the big releases, right in the city centre. Teachers will find school screenings, with a dedicated contact, and event organisers can hire the screens.

## Practical information

- **Opening hours**: every day, Monday to Sunday, from 1 pm to 11:30 pm.
- **Prices**: €12 full price, €10.75 reduced (seniors, students, teachers, job seekers). Article 27 and Cineville Pass accepted.
- **6-seat pass**: valid for 6 months, not personal, for classic screenings, on sale at the box office or on the website.
- **Getting there**: metro 1 and 5, trams 3, 4, 31, 32 and 33 (De Brouckère); Brussels Central station.
- **Contact**: +32 (0)2 219 92 02, info@cinema-aventure.be.
