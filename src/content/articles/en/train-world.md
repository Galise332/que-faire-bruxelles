To get into Train World, you first walk through a station. Schaerbeek's beautiful Art Nouveau station and the museum complex built right next to it house the SNCB railway museum. Here you travel through the past, present and future of Belgian railways, surrounded by a collection the museum itself describes as unique. And there's no untouchable display case at every turn: Train World presents itself as an interactive museum where you can touch (almost) everything.

## What to do there

You follow the history of the Belgian rail network, from its beginnings to the present day, along a route designed to bring you close to the machines rather than looking at them from afar. You can explore freely at your own pace or book a guided tour: it lasts about two hours and takes in the museum's highlights. After the guided tour, you're free to carry on through the rooms on your own.

For a break before or after, the station café at the museum entrance serves light local food: croques-monsieur, soups and drinks.

## Who it's for

Families first and foremost: the museum describes itself as an outing for young and old, where children discover trains in a playful way. School classes, who'll find a trip linked to the curriculum. Groups, associations and clubs, with dedicated rates. And anyone who's kept a soft spot for trains. The museum is fully accessible to people with reduced mobility.

## Practical info

- **Opening hours**: Tuesday to Sunday, 10am to 5pm (last entry at 3:30pm). Closed on Mondays.
- **Exceptional closures**: the Salle des Pas Perdus is inaccessible on 19 November and 4 December; the museum is completely closed on 5 December.
- **Prices**: adults €15; ages 6-17 €11; ages 4-5 €5; free up to and including age 3. Free entry with the Brussels Card.
- **Groups**: from 15 paying adults, €11 per person; guided tour €120 per guide (admission not included), to be booked by email at least three weeks in advance.
- **Getting there**: less than two minutes' walk from Schaerbeek station. Trams 7 and 92, buses 56, 58, 59 and 69, Villo station 160.
- **Train**: the SNCB offers a Discovery Ticket with a discount on the return trip on the day of your visit.
