On the pedestrian stretch of Boulevard Anspach, a stone's throw from the Bourse, the Palace occupies a building that has kept the spirit and codes of Art Nouveau. After more than a century of history, it has returned to its original purpose: welcoming the public to watch films. Today it is an arthouse cinema that aims to be open, welcoming and accessible to as many people as possible, with screens, a bar-restaurant and exhibition spaces under one roof.

## What to do there

You come first for the programme, which focuses on quality and diversity: auteur films, previews, meetings with film crews, festivals and special screenings. The cinema has four screens, from 60 to 373 seats. The large Screen 1 is equipped with a 4K projector but also a 35 mm projector, so some classics can be seen again on film.

Before or after the film, stop by Elvira, the Palace's bar-restaurant, run by the Beautifood team. The menu changes with the seasons, and cinemagoers get 20% off food on the day of their screening when they show their ticket. The restaurant entrance is at Rue Jules Van Praet 28.

The Palace also offers programmes for schools and associations, activities for young audiences, and exhibitions in its spaces.

## Who it's for

Film lovers looking for something other than the big releases, curious visitors who want to discover a heritage building in the city centre, and anyone who likes to pair a film with a meal without changing address. Under-18s have their own rate, and schools and groups have a dedicated offer.

## Practical info

- **Box office**: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 11:15am–10pm; Wednesday 8:45am–10pm; Saturday and Sunday 10:15am–10pm.
- **Elvira bar-restaurant**: Monday 5:30pm–11pm, Tuesday to Saturday 11am–11pm, closed on Sunday (except special events). Bookings on 02 503 58 27.
- **Prices**: €11 full price, €9 reduced (students, seniors, teachers, jobseekers…), €8 for under-18s, €8 before 1pm on weekdays (except public holidays). €1 supplement for films over 2 hours. 6-ticket pass at €42, also valid at Cinéma Vendôme.
- **Booking**: by phone (02 503 57 96), by email or on site, paid at the box office. Bookings not collected 15 minutes before the screening are released for sale.
- **Good to know**: entry to the screens closes 15 minutes after the film starts, and no eating or drinking (except water) inside.
- **Getting there**: Bourse stop (premetro), a few steps away.
