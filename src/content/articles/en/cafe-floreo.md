A stone's throw from Saint-Géry, but slightly away from the bustle, Rue des Riches Claires hides a café that fully embraces its location. Café Floréo describes itself as « off-centre, little-known, in the shadow of the big hubs of activity », and that is exactly what it likes about itself: being the *stam café*, the regulars' café, the kind Brussels does so well. Its terrace looks out onto the Notre-Dame aux Riches Claires church, a fine example of baroque architecture right in the city centre.

## What to do there

You sit down to talk. That is the founding idea of the place: somewhere to share, meet people and unwind, where regulars and tourists mix and where striking up a conversation with the next table feels natural. In a city centre where you often pass lots of people without speaking to them, Floréo sets out to do the opposite.

The menu has been built up over the years, with classics and house creations, at prices meant to be affordable. Those who don't drink alcohol aren't forgotten: the café highlights its homemade herbal lemonades. A food menu rounds it all off, available on the website.

Some evenings, music joins in: the café says it hosts live music and DJs. Dates are announced in the newsletter and on Instagram (@cafefloreo.brussels).

## Who it's for

Brussels locals looking for a neighbourhood café right in the centre, somewhere to come back to often. Visitors who want to get off the busiest streets without going far. Friends meeting up after work, families out for a weekend stroll (the café says it welcomes everyone, « with family, on your own or with friends »), and anyone who loves a terrace with a real view.
