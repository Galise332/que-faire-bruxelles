The Varia has been based in Ixelles since it was founded in 1981, between Place Flagey and the European district. It is one of the six Centres scéniques of the Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles, and its mission fits in one line: to support contemporary Belgian creation in all its diversity. Since July 2021, it has been run by Coline Struyf, alongside 11 partner artists and collectives invited to make the place their home over the long term.

## What to do there

You come to see theatre of today, with a programme the Varia itself describes as « uncompartmentalised and unbridled »: new stories, hybrid forms, artists from varied backgrounds. It all takes place in two venues. The Théâtre Varia, rue du Sceptre, hosts the large productions. The Studio Varia, at 154 rue Gray, is both a rehearsal space and a small hall geared towards more intimate and experimental forms and young talent.

Before or after the show, the Café Ania takes over: local, artisanal food at affordable prices, open with or without a ticket. At the Théâtre, it opens 2 hours before performances that start after 8pm, and 1 hour before the others. At the Studio, only the bar is open, 1 hour before the show.

## Who it's for

Curious minds who want to discover today's Belgian stage, students, and everyone for whom the price of a ticket is a barrier: with the « Pay what you can » system, everyone chooses their own rate. The Varia also offers a selection of shows to see as a family, and meetings with the artists around some of the plays.

## Practical info

- **Addresses**: Théâtre Varia, rue du Sceptre 78, 1050 Ixelles. Studio Varia, rue Gray 154, 1050 Ixelles.
- **« Pay what you can » prices**: at the Théâtre, €13, €17, €22, €27 or €36; at the Studio, €10, €13, €17, €22 or €27. The suggested price is underlined for each show. Non-named Pass Varia: 5 tickets for €60, 11 tickets for €121.
- **Box office**: online until the last minute, by phone on 02 640 35 50 or at the counter from Tuesday to Friday, 10am to 12pm and 1pm to 6pm, and on show nights from 1 hour before. Closed on Mondays and public holidays.
- **Sold-out show**: waiting list on site, 1 hour before the performance.
- **Getting there**: bus 60 (Varia stop), 38 and 95 (Blyckaerts stop), 59 (Natation stop), tram 81 (Germoir stop), metro Schuman or Maelbeek 15 minutes on foot. The theatre recommends public transport.
- **Accessibility**: the Théâtre has ramps, a lift and adapted toilets; the Studio has a platform lift. Let them know in advance via info@varia.be.
