A tightly wrapped rice paper roll, a dipping sauce on the side, and off you go. Knees to Chin describes itself as a place for "rice paper rolls & sidewalk food". The idea is simple: no bookings, you walk in, pick your rolls or a bowl, and eat in or take away. The house is based on Rue de Livourne, near Bailli, and now has five addresses in Brussels.

## What's on the plate

The star is the roll. Every version starts from the same base, rice vermicelli, rocket and white cabbage, then takes on its own character. The **Caramelised Tofu** (€6.40) adds pineapple, pickled red cabbage, vegan hoisin mayonnaise and Thai basil, with a soy-sesame sauce. The **Sweet Potato** (€6.40) relies on fresh tarragon, pickled carrots and cucumbers and a miso mayonnaise, served with peanut sauce. The **Minced Chicken** (€6.70) brings fresh mint and pickled mustard seeds.

For a bigger meal, the seasonal menu offers bowls and noodles at around €15: a rice bowl with roast pork, edamame and dill, chicken noodle soup with satay chicken, noodles with spicy minced pork, or a satay chicken rice bowl (€15.90). These dishes change with the seasons; the full menu is on the website.

## Who it's for

A quick, fresh lunch between two meetings. Vegetarians, who get real options among the rolls. Anyone who wants a light dinner without cooking, since everything can also be ordered to take away. And anyone looking for a small present: gift cards start at €10 and work in every location.

## Practical info

- **Main address**: Rue de Livourne 125-127 (Bailli), open Monday to Sunday from 11:30am to 10pm.
- **Other addresses in Brussels**: Avenue des Celtes 9 (Merode), Rue Francart 18 (Saint-Boniface) and Rue de Flandre 28 (Sainte-Catherine), every day from 11:30am to 10pm; Chaussée d'Alsemberg 148 (Saint-Gilles), Monday to Saturday from 11:30am to 10pm, closed on Sunday.
- **Prices**: rolls from €6.40, seasonal dishes between €15.30 and €15.90.
- **Booking**: none, just turn up. Online ordering available for in-store pickup.
- **Phone**: 02 532 14 35.
