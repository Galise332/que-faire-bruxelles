On Rue de la Tulipe, a short walk from Place Fernand Cocq, an unassuming door leads into one of the best-known jazz clubs in Brussels. The Sounds has been run since 1986 by Sergio Duvalloni and Rosy Merlini, and its website sums it up in one line: "You are not in Chicago or New Orleans, you are in Ixelles." Here the atmosphere, the warmth and the music all carry the colour of authentic jazz, and it has been that way for forty years.

## What to do there

You come to hear musicians up close, in a club on a human scale. In its own words, the Sounds welcomes the best Belgian and international jazz musicians. Concerts start at 9:30pm from Monday to Thursday and at 10pm on Friday and Saturday, so you can arrive early, settle in at a table and wait for the band to take the stage.

The Sounds is also a kitchen: it's Italian, it's Rosy's domain, and it opens at 8pm. That's enough to turn the concert into a proper night out, with dinner before the first set. The club stays open late, until 4am, for those who want to keep the music going.

## Who it's for

Jazz lovers who prefer a club to a big concert hall. Curious newcomers who have never seen a jazz concert live and want to start somewhere warm and welcoming. Couples and groups of friends looking for a weeknight out of the ordinary, with dinner and music under one roof. Ixelles night owls, since the club closes late.

## Practical info

- **Opening hours**: Monday to Saturday from 8pm to 4am. Closed on Sundays.
- **Concerts**: Monday to Thursday at 9:30pm, Friday and Saturday at 10pm.
- **Kitchen**: Italian, open from 8pm.
- **Bus**: lines 54 and 71, Fernand Cocq stop; lines 34, 38, 80 and 95, Idalie stop.
- **Car**: the « Tulipe » car park is close by, first hour free (closed on Sundays and public holidays).
