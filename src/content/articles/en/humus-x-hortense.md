A stone's throw from Place Flagey, a house on rue de Vergnies is home to one of the most singular tables in Brussels. Humus x Hortense presents itself as the first plant-based restaurant in Belgium to have earned a Michelin star. Behind it is a couple: chef Nicolas Decloedt, one of the Maîtres Cuisiniers de Belgique, and Caroline Baerten, a trained chef with a background in art history. Together, they have been talking about "botanical gastronomy" since 2008.

## What to eat

A single tasting menu, entirely plant-based, that changes with what the house calls its 24 micro-seasons. More than 170 regional ingredients pass through it, sourced from regenerative farms, including plants foraged at the wild farm Le Monde des Mille Couleurs. Time is the central ingredient: preservation techniques, slow fermentations, textures that evolve. For drinks, Caroline Baerten devises botanical pairings, with a homemade alcohol-free version. Even the tableware is made in-house: the ceramics are shaped by Caroline, and the interior showcases Belgian design and craftsmanship.

The work has been recognised: on top of the star, Gault & Millau named the house "Best Plant-Based Menu of the Year" 2026, and the restaurant ranks 9th in a worldwide Top 100 of vegetarian restaurants.

## Who it's for

For celebrating an occasion, discovering just how far plant-based cooking can go, or treating someone to an evening out of the ordinary (gift vouchers are on sale on the website). The curious who are still hesitating can start with the pre-theatre menu or the Friday and Saturday lunch.

## Practical info

- **Lunch**: Friday and Saturday, from 12pm to 2:30pm. Kronos menu (4 courses) €97, tasting menu (6 courses) €127.
- **Dinner**: pre-theatre menu (4 courses) €85, for arrival between 5:30pm and 6pm, table until 7:30pm; tasting menu €155 (6 courses) or €180 (8 courses). On Friday and Saturday evenings, only the Kairos and Ceraon menus (with pairings) are served.
- **Pairings**: small pairing €48, large pairing €75, with or without alcohol.
- **Booking**: online for 1 to 5 people, by email from 6. A guarantee is required, with free cancellation up to 48 hours before.
- **Allergies**: to be reported in detail at least 24 hours in advance, as there is only one menu.
- **Getting there**: secure underground car park on Place Flagey, a 2-minute walk away.
- **Phone**: +32 474 65 37 06.
