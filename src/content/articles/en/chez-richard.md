On Rue des Minimes, in the Antiques quarter, Chez Richard occupies a building that went up in 1890. Refurbished by its team, the bistro opens every day from 11:30am to midnight and takes no reservations: you drop by, see if there's a table free and sit down. That simple, no-appointment rhythm has made it a meeting place for the neighbourhood.

## What's on the menu

The menu changes with the time of day. Lunch runs from noon to 3pm. On weekdays the board lists a dish of the day and a vegetarian suggestion, alongside classics such as mussels and fries (€25), a cut of beef with the sauce of the day and fries (€23), or soup of the day with a filled baguette (€14). At weekends you'll find a medium-rare burger, américain with fries or salmon gravlax. Cheese and shrimp croquettes are on the lunch menu all week.

From 3:30pm it's time for quick bites: a pot of fries with homemade mayonnaise (€6.50), a fricandelle with Richard sauce, croquettes by the piece. From 5:30pm to 10pm come the sharing plates: bouquet prawns to peel yourself, beef carpaccio, burrata with confit tomatoes, artichoke vinaigrette, hummus with confit garlic and mushrooms. On Saturdays and Sundays, from noon, the seafood counter offers oysters, whelks and grey shrimp.

The bistro highlights produce from Brussels, Belgium or France: Dierendonck for meat, De Jager for fish and seafood, Wittamer and Dandoy for sweet things. Alcohol-free options on the menu include a homemade iced tea and an organic farm apple juice.

## Who it's for

For a break during a stroll through the Antiques quarter, a lunch with no need to book, or a light evening meal with friends, sharing several plates. The terrace closes at 11pm, an hour before the dining room.

## Practical info

- **Hours**: every day, 11:30am to midnight. Terrace closes at 11pm.
- **Kitchen**: lunch from noon to 3pm, quick bites from 3:30pm, evening menu from 5:30pm to 10pm.
- **Prices**: according to the online menu, pot of fries €6.50, soup of the day with a filled baguette €14 (weekday lunch), mussels and fries €25.
- **Reservations**: not taken.
