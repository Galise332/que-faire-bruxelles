On Rue Braemt in Saint-Josse, a former brewery has been home to a theatre since 1994. That year, two artists, Patricia Ide and Michel Kacenelenbogen, moved Le Public in and turned the building into a performance space, with help from the set designer and architect Luc D'Haenens. The project started with a single stage, initially on their own funds. Today it has three.

## What to do there

You come here for theatre, in very different forms. Le Public stands for work that is, in its own words, "popular and unifying": contemporary pieces as much as the great classics, with plenty of room for writers, actors and artists from Belgium. The house describes its programme as a balance "between a smile and reflection". It changes through the season, and the calendar on the website shows what's on.

The evening can start before the show. For an 8:30 pm performance, the theatre opens from 6:30 pm, with a bar, a bookshop and a restaurant. The restaurant offers a €35 set menu and is limited to 60 covers per service, so booking is strongly recommended.

The offer to remember is called "à tout prix". On Thursdays and Sundays, everyone picks their own price between €5 and €50, with no need to justify it. These seats can be booked from the Monday before the performance.

## Who it's for

Theatre lovers, people keen to discover Belgian artists, and anyone on a tight budget, thanks to the "à tout prix" evenings and youth rates. Groups of at least ten people get their own rate too. Audio description is offered with Audioscenic ASBL for blind and visually impaired people.

On access, the theatre is upfront: every stage is reached by steps or stairs, and there is no lift. Access for people with reduced mobility is difficult but possible, except with an electric wheelchair.

## Practical info

- **Performances**: 8:30 pm on Tuesdays, Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays, 7 pm on Wednesdays, 5 pm on Sundays, with some exceptions. Latecomers can't enter once the show has started.
- **Prices** (2025-2026 rates shown on the website): €31 adults, €28 aged 65 and over, €20 under 30 and €10 under 24, with proof of age for the last two.
- **Booking**: online or on 02 724 24 44, Monday to Friday from 12 pm to 6 pm and Saturday from 2 pm to 6 pm, except public holidays.
- **Getting there**: metro Madou (lines 2 and 6) and Maelbeek (lines 1 and 5), buses 29, 59, 61, 63, 65 and 66.
