A stone's throw from the Fish Market, behind a shopfront on Rue de Flandre, you step into a large 100 m² workshop where every table is already set: vials and pipettes on one side, beads and pliers on the other, blocks of clay further along. At Break-it, you come to make something with your hands for an hour or two, and you take it home with you.

## What you do there

Five workshops, all materials included, run in French or English:

- **Perfume making**: 1h, you compose your own fragrance from top, heart and base notes, and leave with your bottle.
- **Jewellery making**: 2h, rings, bracelets or necklaces, depending on what you feel like wearing.
- **Candle making**: 2h15, wax, scent and container of your choice.
- **Air-dry clay**: 2h, an object you shape yourself and that dries without an oven.
- **Mosaic**: 2h30, the longest workshop, for a trivet or a small picture.

No skills are required, the team guides you through every step. Groups can reach 30 or 40 people depending on the workshop, and Break-it also comes to companies or to your home anywhere in Belgium.

## Who it's for

The date night that makes a change from the restaurant, a teenager's or an adult's birthday, the Saturday hen or stag party, and above all teams: creative team building has become the house speciality, with the option to privatise the entire workshop.

## Practical information

- **Opening hours**: Monday to Friday from 1 pm to 8 pm, weekends from 10 am to 8 pm.
- **Price per person**: perfume €70, jewellery €35, candles €55, clay €35, mosaic €60, materials included.
- **Booking**: not needed if you come alone, as a pair or in a small group. Mandatory from 6 people and for off-site workshops, via the form on the website. Free quote within 24 hours.
- **Metro**: Sainte-Catherine (lines 1 and 5), a 3-minute walk.
