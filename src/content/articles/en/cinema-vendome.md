The Vendôme opened in October 1952, when Henry Fol renamed a small neighbourhood cinema near Louise. From the very start, it championed a more intimate, quality cinema. In 1977, it became one of the first multiplexes of its time, with Sophia Loren as its godmother. Driven out by soaring rents in 1991, it was reborn a year later, almost to the day, on chaussée de Wavre, in the former Le Roy cinema, a stone's throw from Porte de Namur. Since 1995, it has had five screens and nearly 900 seats.

## What to do there

You come to see films chosen for their quality, from every corner of the world, on national release and in their original version. The line is clear: championing arthouse cinema, Belgian and European cinema, and introducing audiences to young directors and first films. The cinema describes itself as a « Galerie d'Art Cinématographique » (a gallery of cinematic art).

All year round, the Vendôme also hosts festivals, some of them now in their tenth edition, as well as previews. It offers screenings for schools and rents out its screens to associations and companies.

## Who it's for

Film lovers who want to get away from blockbusters, curious minds who like to see the world through other windows, and those on a tight budget: weekday screenings before 3pm cost €8 for everyone. Under-18s also pay €8.

## Practical info

- **Screenings**: according to the programme, available on the website.
- **Prices**: €11 full price. €8 for everyone Monday to Friday before 3pm (excluding public holidays). Otherwise, €9 for seniors, students (18-26), teachers and jobseekers, €8 for under-18s, with proof. Loyalty card for 6 screenings at €42. Article 27 and Cineville accepted.
- **Tickets**: online on the Cinéshop Vendôme up to 2 hours before the screening, or on site without booking, subject to availability. Tickets cannot be exchanged or refunded.
- **Getting there**: metro 2 and 6, Porte de Namur or Louise stop; trams 92, 94 and 97, Louise stop; buses 54 and 71, Matongé stop.
- **Parking**: the 2 Portes car park, a 2-minute walk away, offers a reduced rate with a voucher available from the box office (subject to availability).
- **Contact**: 02 502 37 00.
