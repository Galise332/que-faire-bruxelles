In Etterbeek, in the Jourdan district, in the shadow of the European institutions, Stirwen stands for a simple idea: cook with the season and respect the produce. In the kitchen is chef François-Xavier Lambory, named Young Chef of the Year Brussels 2022 by Gault & Millau. His cooking is classic fine dining, faithful to tradition and open to invention, served in a setting the house aims to keep elegant and comfortable.

## What's on the menu

The menu follows the calendar, and the house says it has built a close relationship with its suppliers over the years. The November 2026 menu, called "Brumaire", puts game centre stage: roe deer, hind, mallard, partridge and hare. You will also find wild mushrooms, squash and pumpkin, scallops from Port-en-Bessin, Brittany brown crab, and fish from Brittany and Normandy, depending on the day's catch.

If you would rather be guided, there are the "découverte de saison" menus, served to the whole table. The chef composes a surprise menu of 4 to 8 courses, based on your tastes and on what arrives that day.

## Who it's for

Lovers of fine dining and game, and anyone looking for a beautiful table to mark an occasion. Stirwen also hosts business meals, seminars and family events such as a birthday or a family reunion: the private room seats up to 24 people, and the whole restaurant can be booked. The house also offers catering and gift vouchers.

## Practical info

- **Opening times**: lunch Wednesday to Friday (June to August, Fridays only); dinner Tuesday to Saturday. Groups and banquets any day, on request.
- **Prices** (November 2026 menu): starters from €42 to €52, mains from €55 to €75. Discovery menus: €90 (4 courses, not on Saturday evenings), €110 (5), €130 (6), €175 (8).
- **Booking**: form on the website, info@stirwen.be or +32 2 640 85 41.
- **Parking**: public car park under Place Jourdan, entrance at Avenue du Maelbeek 18.
- **Allergens**: list available on request.
