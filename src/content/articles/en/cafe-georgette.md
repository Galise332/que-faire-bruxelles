Two streets from the Grand-Place, in the maze of the Îlot Sacré, rue de la Fourche hides a place that refuses to pick a side. Café Georgette describes itself as neither a chip shop, nor a restaurant, nor a café, but a mix of all three. You come for the fries, you stay for a proper meal, and before long you find yourself leafing through the little Brussels dialect glossary tucked in among the menus.

## What to eat

Fries first, since they are the soul of the house: real Belgian fries, cooked in beef fat. Around them, a brasserie menu that proudly claims its "Belgitude", with meat at its heart. The house announces pasture-raised beef, organic free-range pork guaranteed antibiotic-free and Walloon Mulard duck. On the menu: plancha grills, minced-meat burgers, américain and carpaccio. In the kitchen, they cook with butter.

A drinks list rounds it all off, and the small Brussels glossary lets you brush up on the local dialect between bites.

## Who it's for

For visitors who want to taste Belgian fries worthy of the name right in the historic centre. For the group of friends torn between a cone on the go and a real dish. For meat lovers who like to know where it comes from. Open every day non-stop from noon, Georgette is also a handy option for an off-hours meal after a stroll through the centre.

## Practical info

- **Opening hours**: every day from 12pm, until 10pm Sunday to Thursday and until 11pm on Friday and Saturday.
- **Payment**: cash, Visa, Mastercard, debit card and meal vouchers accepted.
- **On site**: terrace and bike parking.
- **Phone**: +32 2 512 18 12.
- **Getting there**: a few minutes' walk from the Grand-Place; De Brouckère and Bourse – Grand-Place stops are close by.
