Marble tables, a wood-fired oven, a dining room that fills up fast and music that gets louder as the weekend approaches: NONA Pizza St Cat is a little slice of Naples a stone's throw from Sainte-Catherine church. Opened in 2017, it is NONA's very first restaurant, which has since grown into a small family of pizzerias and fresh pasta restaurants in Brussels. There are no reservations here: you turn up, wait for a table and eat a pizza made by the rules of Naples.

## What to eat

Neapolitan pizza, the real thing. The restaurant is certified by the Associazione Verace Pizza Napoletana (AVPN), which protects the original recipe: long-matured dough, a puffy rim (the famous *cornicione*) and a very short bake. Look for plaque no. 699 as you walk in.

The dough is made every day in the NONA atelier, and the toppings follow the seasons. NONA works with organic Belgian vegetable farmers and only brings from Italy what it can't find in Belgium, from small producers. On the menu: the classics (marinara, margherita, bufala), a vegan autumn pizza with pumpkin cream, and monthly specials. For dessert, tiramisu or panna cotta with a seasonal fruit coulis.

## Who it's for

Pizza lovers who want dough worthy of the name, families (there's a kids' combo for under-10s), and groups of friends who enjoy a lively atmosphere. If you're after a quiet dinner for two, come on a weekday or early in the evening: on Fridays and Saturdays it's loud and buzzing, and that's the point.

## Practical info

- **Opening hours**: every day, from 12 noon.
- **Indicative prices**: marinara €10, margherita €12.50, kids' combo (baby margherita + drink) €12.10, tiramisu €6.90.
- **Reservations**: not possible, walk-in only.
- **Vegetarian / vegan**: several pizzas marked on the menu.
- **Metro**: Sainte-Catherine (lines 1 and 5), a two-minute walk.
