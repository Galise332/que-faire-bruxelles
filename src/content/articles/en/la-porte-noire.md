From Rue des Alexiens, all you see is a door. Behind it lies a vaulted room that, in the 16th century, served as the kitchens of the Alexian monastery. The monks are long gone, the vaults have stayed, and La Porte Noire has turned them into a bar with a medieval and Celtic spirit, set to a rock'n'roll soundtrack. It is one of those Brussels addresses where the setting makes half the evening.

## What you do there

You settle in under the old stones and chat with friends, in an atmosphere that makes you want to stay. The menu puts Belgian craft first, with more than 120 beers from the country's breweries, 12 of them on tap, as well as soft drinks and fruit juices for those who don't drink alcohol.

The cellar also lives to the rhythm of its programme: concerts every other Thursday, DJ sets every Saturday and other evenings announced on the website. The bar even invites its customers to suggest the artists they would like to see on its stage. Another detail that says a lot about how attached the regulars are: the place was backed by a crowdfunding campaign, and the campaign's t-shirts and sweatshirts are still available at the bar.

## Who it's for

Lovers of places steeped in history, rock fans, groups of friends looking for an out-of-the-ordinary night in the centre, and the curious who want to see what the basement of a Brussels monastery looked like. It is an evening bar, designed for adults.

## Practical info

- **Hours**: Monday to Thursday from 6pm to 1am, Friday and Saturday from 6pm to 3am.
- **Prices**: soft drinks and juices from €2, Belgian beers from around €4 (full menu on the website).
- **Concerts**: every other Thursday, DJ sets on Saturdays. Programme on laportenoire.be.
- **Contact**: by phone on +32 2 428 95 06 or via the form on the website.
