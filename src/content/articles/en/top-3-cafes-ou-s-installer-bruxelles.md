To sit down for a while with a coffee, read or open your laptop, you still have to pick the right table. Here are three addresses with very different personalities: a place that says openly you can work there, a roaster with a bar that opens at 7am and a fully plant-based café in the Marolles. One note: their websites say nothing about wifi or sockets, so ask when you arrive if you are bringing a laptop.

## 1. Lazy Bear

**18/20 rue de l'Épée, 1000 Bruxelles** · [lazybear.be](https://www.lazybear.be/)

The house sums up its spirit in four verbs: eat, drink, work and enjoy. In the heart of Brussels, Lazy Bear presents itself as a place where you can work as easily as unwind, with burgers and an atmosphere it wants to be vibrant and welcoming. On Saturdays and Sundays, late in the morning, it is brunch time, with pancakes, a French toast skewer and avocado toast. Winter hours listed on the website: 10am to 7pm Tuesday to Thursday and on Sunday, 10am to 8pm on Friday and Saturday. You can book through the website.

## 2. Belga & Co

**Rue du Bailli 7A, 1000 Bruxelles** · **Rue Ernest Solvay 12, 1050 Ixelles** · **Avenue Brugmann 2, 1060 Bruxelles** · [belgacoffee.com](https://www.belgacoffee.com/)

Belga & Co roasts its coffee in Antwerp, every week to guarantee freshness, and serves it in three Brussels bars. For the house, coffee is a moment when the routine stops and you put your worries aside. The earliest riser is the bar on Avenue Brugmann: open 7am to 6pm on weekdays and 8:30am to 6pm at weekends. On Rue du Bailli and Rue Ernest Solvay, it is 8am to 6pm on weekdays and 9am to 6pm at weekends. With the Efico Foundation, the brand also supports more than 50 sustainable projects in producing countries.

## 3. Taylor's Café

**Rue Haute 228, 1000 Bruxelles** · [taylorscafebrussels.be](https://www.taylorscafebrussels.be/)

Opened in December 2021 in the Marolles, Taylor's Café is entirely plant-based. Its founder, Lydia Taylor, discovered the world of cosy cafés and tea rooms while studying in the UK, and set up the project with the help of her sister Rachel. Local suppliers, seasonal products, no single-use plastic: sustainability is at the heart of the place. Open 9am to 4pm on Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday, 10am to 5pm on Saturday and Sunday, closed on Tuesday. The café does not take reservations: just drop by.
