In Brussels, fries aren't necessarily eaten in a restaurant: you get them at the counter, in a paper cone, and eat them standing up or on a bench. Here are three friteries that have been going for decades, each in a different neighbourhood, all open 7 days a week until 1 am. Enough to fit in a cone after an exhibition, a film or a walk, in November as in July.

## 1. Maison Antoine

**Place Jourdan 1, 1040 Etterbeek** · [maisonantoine.be](http://maisonantoine.be/)

The best known in the city, on Place Jourdan since 1948 and now run by the third generation. The kiosk, with its illuminated sign, is part of the landscape of the European Quarter. Open 7 days a week from 11:30 am to 1 am. The website also shows the menu and a catering service for events.

## 2. Friterie de la Barrière

**Rue Fontainas 1, 1060 Saint-Gilles** · [friteriedelabarriere.be](https://www.friteriedelabarriere.be/)

A Saint-Gilles institution for more than 60 years, at the Barrière de Saint-Gilles. Every day, Vasile and his team make fresh fries, cut by hand and cooked in beef fat. Open every day from 11:30 am to 1 am. Handy for finishing a walk in upper Saint-Gilles.

## 3. Fritland

**Rue Henri Maus 49, 1000 Bruxelles** · [fritlandbrussels.be](https://fritlandbrussels.be/)

A stone's throw from the Bourse, Fritland has been serving Belgian fries since 1978. It's the city-centre address when you're wandering between the Grand-Place and the boulevards: homemade fries with sauce, but also burgers, hot dogs, sandwiches and snacks for those who want more than a cone. Open every day from 11 am to 1 am, with quick service.
