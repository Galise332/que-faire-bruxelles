In 2017, Cédric Mosbeux opened Fernand Obb Delicatessen in a former hair salon on Rue de Tamines, in Saint-Gilles. A year later, his grey shrimp croquette was named the best in Brussels at a contest held at Comme chez Soi. In 2019, it won again. Since then, this small counter serving Belgian comfort food has become an address Brussels locals pass on to each other.

## What to eat

The star is the croquette, made entirely in-house: grey shrimp, or cheese made with Flandrien, a semi-aged Gouda from the Kortrijk area. Have one on its own, as a pair or combined, on site or to cook at home.

Alongside, there are burgers (beef, breaded free-range chicken, breaded fish, Belgian halloumi, homemade veggie patty, white or black pudding from an artisan butcher), the homemade fricadelle and américain préparé. The house also smokes its own pastrami from Belgian beef brisket and its Label Rouge Scottish salmon, sold by weight to take away or eaten on site. Ingredients are Belgian and everything is prepared in the house kitchens or by local artisans.

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants to taste a real shrimp croquette without going to a big restaurant. Families and groups of friends looking for a simple, generous meal. Food lovers who leave with croquettes to cook at home. The atmosphere is relaxed, and Gault & Millau gave it the "Pop of the Year 2020" award.
