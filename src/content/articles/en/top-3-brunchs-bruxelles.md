In Brussels, brunch sometimes needs booking like a holiday dinner. Here are three very different weekend addresses: a big buffet in a cultural venue, a set brunch in a space designed with families in mind, and a specialty coffee shop known for its pancakes.

## 1. La Tricoterie

**Rue Théodore Verhaegen 158, 1060 Saint-Gilles** · [tricoterie.be](https://www.tricoterie.be/brunch/)

This 1,600 m² cultural venue, founded in 2010, serves an all-you-can-eat sweet and savoury buffet every Sunday, homemade with organic and local produce: waffles, muffins, crêpes, eggs and bacon, and a spread that changes each week. It costs €29 per adult, €12 for ages 7 to 12, €6 for ages 4 to 6, and is free up to age 3. Water, juice and tea are unlimited, and one coffee or hot chocolate is included. There are two sittings, 10:00 to 12:15 and 12:30 to 14:30, except on Sundays during school holidays. Children aged 3 and up have their own activity, the Mini-Tricoteurs. Book using the form on the website; for information, call +32 2 537 96 69.

## 2. Garage-à-Manger

**Rue Washington 185, 1050 Ixelles** · [garage-a-manger.be](https://garage-a-manger.be/)

Expect sofas and armchairs for the grown-ups, a caravan and books for the little ones, and a direct connection to the Pêle-Mêle bookshop. The set brunch is served on Saturdays and Sundays for €28. The kitchen focuses on seasonal fruit and vegetables, and younger guests (12 and under) have their own €13 set meal. Open 10:30 to 18:00 on Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday, 10:30 to 20:00 on Friday and Saturday, closed on Tuesday. Book on +32 2 880 67 74.

## 3. Kafei

**Chaussée de Vleurgat 147, Bruxelles** and **Rue Antoine Dansaert 57, 1000 Bruxelles** · [kafei.be](https://www.kafei.be/)

Started in 2019 by Belgian-born Chinese siblings Ping, Mickael and Stephen, Kafei ("coffee" in pinyin) built its name on specialty coffee, fluffy pancakes and a brunch of eggs Benedict. It opens Tuesday to Friday from 10:00 to 17:30 on Chaussée de Vleurgat and Monday to Friday at the same times on Rue Dansaert, with both addresses open from 10:00 to 17:00 on Saturday and Sunday. The Chaussée de Vleurgat shop is closed on Monday. As Kafei itself says, it's best to book for the weekend, sometimes weeks ahead.
