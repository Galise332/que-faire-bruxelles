You know the picture: Big Ben at knee height with the spheres of the Atomium behind it. That's Mini-Europe, but on a route that easily takes two hours if you take the time to trigger the animations scattered along the paths.

## What you do there

The park presents the monuments of the countries of the European Union reproduced in miniature, in gardens maintained all year round, with hyper-realistic settings. An interactive exhibition, "Spirit of Europe", explains how the Union works to children (and to adults who no longer dared to ask). There is a restaurant on site, and combined visits are available with the neighbouring attractions.

## Who it's for

Families with children aged 5 to 12 first and foremost. Groups and school classes. Brussels locals who haven't set foot there since primary school.

## Practical info

- **Opening hours**: every day from 9:30am to 6pm, last entry at 5pm.
- **Prices**: adult from €21.70 online (€25 on site), child under 12 from €15.30 online (€17.90 on site), free under 115 cm.
- **Metro**: Heysel (line 6), then a 5-minute walk.
- **Tip**: book online for the reduced rate and to avoid the queue at weekends.
