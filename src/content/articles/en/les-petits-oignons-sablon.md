The story begins in 1986 in the Marolles, when Frieda and Khaled Zoughlami took over the former Jardin des Marolles and renamed it Les Petits Oignons. Since 2011, the restaurant has been at number 25 Rue de la Régence, a few steps from the Sablon. It has changed address and owners, but not direction: generous brasserie cooking, fresh produce and a real sense of hospitality. For its 40th birthday in 2026, it treated itself to a new interior: deep blue, wood, cognac leather and marble, designed with Studio Idealist.

## What to eat

The menu mixes great Belgian classics, French tradition and Mediterranean touches. It changes with the seasons, but some dishes never leave: the North Sea grey shrimp croquettes, peeled by hand, the sole meunière and the américain with fries. In 2022 the croquette won the "Coup de cœur" prize at the contest for the best shrimp croquette in Brussels, awarded after a blind tasting.

As for produce, the restaurant says meat and vegetables arrive every morning, and fish twice a day. It is listed in the Michelin Guide.

## Who it's for

Lunch after a stroll around the Sablon or the Petit Sablon, a family dinner that wants Belgian food with no surprises, a birthday meal in a slightly smarter setting, or a visitor you want to introduce to a real shrimp croquette. Since the brasserie opens every day, it is also a safe bet on Sundays and Mondays, when many places in the area are closed.
