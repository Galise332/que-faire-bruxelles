In the shadow of Saint-Boniface church, in Ixelles, Ultime Atome has been welcoming Brussels locals and passers-by since 1986. It describes itself as an "almost timeless" refuge: slate tables, a reworked Art Nouveau setting, a cosmopolitan atmosphere and a terrace. You can come for a full meal, a drink with friends or just a coffee, and that is exactly what makes it worth knowing.

## What to do there

The drinks list goes well beyond beer: coffee, cappuccino, Belgian hot chocolate, homemade lemonade, freshly squeezed juices, smoothies such as the Popeye (spinach, apple, lemon, cucumber, ginger) and mocktails. Adults will also find a wide choice of Belgian beers.

On the plate, this Franco-Belgian brasserie sticks up for the classics, with new suggestions every day: croquettes with grey North Sea shrimps, vol-au-vent, Liège-style meatballs, moules marinière, chicory gratin and Flemish beef carbonnade, which won a bronze medal at a competition in 2024. The fries are fresh. For a group, the "Best of de Belgique" brings together vol-au-vent, carbonnade and Liège-style meatballs. To finish, choose between a Brussels waffle, a dame blanche or Belgian chocolate mousse.

At the moment, a winter brunch is served every Sunday. The menus include vegetarian, lactose-free and gluten-free options.

## Who it's for

Colleagues who want a lunch with quick service. Friends settling in for the evening. Families, for whom the house says it is ideal. Visitors who want to try Belgian dishes in a typically Brussels atmosphere.
