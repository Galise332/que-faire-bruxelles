In the heart of Châtelain, in Ixelles, Glaçage offers a very simple activity: you sit down at a table, choose a ceramic piece, paint it, then come back about three weeks later to collect it, glazed and fired, ready for everyday use. You don't need to know how to draw: the studio welcomes all levels.

## What you do there

The workshop lasts 2.5 hours, with three time slots a day. First you choose your piece from more than 80 models: mug, bowl, plate, carafe. The price depends on the piece, from €10 to €70, and includes materials, glazing and firing. Aprons are provided.

Then comes the painting. One coat gives a translucent effect, several coats a rich, opaque finish. The paints are water-based and non-toxic. While it dries, you take a break: the studio offers drinks and a selection of savoury and sweet bites, with gluten-free options. You can't bring your own food.

The team then takes care of the glazing and firing at 1005 °C. The studio keeps pieces for one month after firing. Express firing within 72 hours is available for an extra €5 per piece. At home, the piece can go in the dishwasher at up to 60 °C; the microwave is strongly discouraged.

Prefer to paint somewhere else? The Glaçage On the Go kit lends you the materials free of charge for 5 days, with a €50 deposit returned when you bring the pieces back. You only pay for the pieces.

## Who it's for

For everyone, alone or in a group: the studio hosts from 1 to 36 people. Children are welcome, with no minimum age (from age 10 for birthday parties in Brussels). Dogs are allowed as long as they stay calm. A good idea for a family outing, an afternoon with friends or a rainy day.
