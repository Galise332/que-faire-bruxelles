You can't miss it: the big building towering over Place Flagey is the former home of Belgian national radio. Designed by architect Joseph Diongre and completed in 1938, nicknamed ‘the liner’, it was partly listed in 1994 and then saved from demolition. Today Flagey is a Brussels cultural institution devoted to music and the moving image, right next to the Ixelles Ponds.

## What to do there

Come for a concert: classical and contemporary music, jazz, traditional music. Studio 4, renovated in the 1990s by Philippe Samyn, is praised for its acoustics, and the Brussels Philharmonic is among the orchestras you can hear there. Flagey also runs festivals such as the Flagey Piano Days and the Brussels Jazz Festival.

Come for a film too, at the Studio 5 cinema. On Saturdays, the ‘Samedis d'Anima’ show animated films for children, and some concerts are designed for families (‘Family Concert’, ages 6 and up).

Before or after the show, the main bar in the ground-floor lobby opens one hour before each concert. A brasserie, Le Variétés, is also in the building, next to the box office.

## Who it's for

Music lovers, from the curious to the devoted. Film fans. Families, thanks to the junior screenings and concerts. And anyone who loves art deco architecture: the building alone is worth the trip.

## Practical info

- **Box office**: to the right of the main entrance, Place Sainte-Croix. Tuesday to Friday, 12pm to 5pm, and one hour before each event (also on Mondays, weekends and public holidays). Phone: 02 641 10 20.
- **Prices**: depend on the event, see the website. Example: €9 for a Samedis d'Anima film, €7 for under-12s. For Brussels Philharmonic concerts, €5 for under-18s and €15 for under-30s. Cineflagey card: 5 films for €38. Booking fee: €1 per ticket.
- **Getting there**: buses 38, 59, 60 and 71; tram 81 (suspended during the Chaussée de Mons roadworks); Germoir station (S5, S9); Villo bike station on the square.
- **Good to know**: latecomers are only let in during applause or the interval.
