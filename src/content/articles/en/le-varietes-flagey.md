On the ground floor of the Flagey “ocean liner”, the legendary building designed by Belgian architect Joseph Diongre in the 1930s, Le Variétés keeps its chickens turning on the spit. This Ixelles brasserie-rotisserie has kept its vintage décor exactly as it was: walls clad in zebrano wood and an L-shaped room that opens onto the bar and the kitchen.

## What you'll eat

The great speciality is spit-roasted farm chicken. You order a quarter or a half per person, with fries and salad, in three versions: Sambre et Meuse sauce, cream of morels or compote.

Around it, the menu sets the great Belgian classics alongside house specialities: homemade grey shrimp croquettes, cheese croquettes, chicken vol-au-vent, américain with fries, Dierendonck white pudding. For vegetarians, there is roasted cauliflower with baba ganoush and hazelnuts, or a fresh quinoa dish with avocado, coriander and lime. To finish: dame blanche Variétés, the chef's chocolate mousse or a red fruit pavlova. The lemonade, orangeade and iced tea are all homemade.

## Who it's for

Anyone craving proper chicken and fries in a handsome setting. A meal before a concert or a film at Flagey, in the same building. A weekday lunch. And architecture lovers, who get to discover an Ixelles landmark from a table.

## Practical info

- **Address**: Place Sainte-Croix 4, 1050 Ixelles, next to Place Flagey.
- **Hours**: kitchen open every day, 12pm to 2pm and 6pm to 10pm.
- **Prices** (menu published on the website): farm chicken from €25 to €35 depending on sauce and portion, main courses from €24 to €42.
- **Lunch**: starter and main for €36, set menu Monday to Friday at midday. Ask your server for the blackboard.
- **Booking**: on the website or at +32 2 320 10 00. After 15 minutes' delay, your table is no longer guaranteed.
- **Groups**: 10 people maximum. From 8 people, a reduced menu or a single menu for the whole table.
- **Dogs**: not allowed, even small ones.
- **Getting there**: buses 38, 59, 60 and 71, Villo station on the square (information published by Flagey).
