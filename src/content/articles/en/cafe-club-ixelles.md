"Come for the coffee, stay for the beers!": Café Club sums up its own concept in a single sentence. On rue de l'Aqueduc, in Ixelles, this neighbourhood café changes character as the day goes on. In the morning, people drop in for a cappuccino and a croissant; at lunchtime, a salad or a croque; in the evening, friends gather around small plates to share. There's no booking: you just walk in and find a seat.

## What to eat

The menu follows the clock. In the morning there are pastries (croissant or pain au chocolat), "Bamboo" granola with Greek yoghurt and white peach purée, banana and dark chocolate pancakes, fried or scrambled eggs, and a ham and cheese croissant.

At lunchtime (12 pm to 2:30 pm, and 12 pm to 4 pm Friday to Sunday), it's time for salads such as the Chicken Club or the Winter Bowl with einkorn and roasted vegetables, burrata or guacamole toasts, croques (Original with comté and ham on the bone, Double cheese with cheddar and brie) and a few dishes like steak tartare with fries or meatballs in tomato sauce. In winter, there's a soup of the day.

In the evening, the kitchen switches to "nibbles" mode: nachos with cheddar, 12-month comté with bread and butter, sweet potato fries, mini croquettes with Vieux Bruges, and a veggie section with chickpea-carrot hummus, oven-baked aubergines or burrata with roasted tomatoes.

As for drinks, coffee takes centre stage: espresso, flat white, latte, chai latte, matcha latte, hot chocolate made with Callebaut chocolate chips, teas and fresh mint infusion. There are also freshly squeezed juices during the day, iced tea and a homemade lemon-ginger lemonade.

## Who it's for

Anyone looking for a café to settle into in lower Ixelles, from breakfast through to the evening. Friends who want to share a few plates without booking. Weekend foodies, who can make the most of the lunch service running until 4 pm.
