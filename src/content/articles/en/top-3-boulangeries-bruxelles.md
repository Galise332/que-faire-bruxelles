Ixelles is a good neighbourhood for bread and sweet treats. Three spots a few streets apart, which you can walk between on a Sunday morning: a sourdough bakery, a workshop you can watch at work through the window and a little shop that makes nothing but cookies.

## 1. Renard Bakery

**Place Fernand Cocq 3, 1050 Ixelles** · [renardbakery.com](https://www.renardbakery.com/fr_BE)

Laurent and Sophie took over the bakery in 2015 and built it around a simple idea: flour, water, sourdough starter and the time to let the dough rise. You'll find rustic sourdough loaves and pastries, laid out on a large old-fashioned baker's table, along with Brussels honey, homemade jams and tea. Open 7 days a week: 7 am to 6:30 pm on weekdays and Saturdays, 7 am to 2 pm on Sundays. You can order online, but not by phone or email.

## 2. Grain

**Rue de l'Aqueduc 84, 1050 Ixelles** · [grainbakery.be](https://grainbakery.be/)

Born in the Châtelain area, Grain now has three shops: rue de l'Aqueduc, rue Lesbroussart 27 (Flagey) and chaussée de Charleroi 174 in Saint-Gilles, the last one with a terrace. What sets it apart: the workshop is open, so you can see the dough being worked by hand and the ovens running. As for prices, the website lists the croissant at €2, the pain au chocolat at €2.20 and the seeded sourdough loaf at €7. Open Wednesday to Sunday, 8 am to 6 pm.

## 3. Chez Martha – Art & Cookies

**Rue Lesbroussart 96, 1050 Ixelles** · [chezmartha.be](https://www.chezmartha.be/)

Half art gallery, half cookie shop: Chez Martha bakes its cookies on site every day, and you can see how they're made. The menu includes vegan and gluten-free cookies, and the shop doesn't use peanuts. All cookies cost the same: €4 each, €22 for six. Takeaway only. Open Tuesday to Saturday from 11 am to 6 pm, Sunday from 11 am to 5:30 pm, closed on Mondays and public holidays.
