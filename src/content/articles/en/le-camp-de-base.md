On Chaussée de Boondael in Ixelles, an old warehouse has become one of the favourite haunts of Brussels climbers. Le Camp de Base is a bouldering gym: no rope, no harness, walls a few metres high above thick mats, and problems in every colour to work out alone or with friends.

## What you do there

Bouldering mixes strength, balance and coordination, but above all thinking: every problem is a small puzzle. The big, bright hall has problems for every level, from absolute beginners to seasoned athletes. Alongside it you'll find a warm-up and conditioning area with pull-up bars and mats, a spray wall and a Güllich board for training, and a quiet corner for stretching.

You don't need a class to start; the staff are happy to give advice on your first session. If you want to learn the right reflexes (falling, foot placement, reading a problem), you can book a 1h30 intro class, then move on to four-session courses or year-long classes. After climbing, the bar corner has something to nibble: toasties, salads, cookies, fruit, and a pizza night on Wednesdays from 6:30 pm.

Le Camp de Base has two little sisters in Ixelles: Le Camp d'Approche (rue du Vivier 53), an introductory gym designed for beginners, children and families, and right next to it Le Camp Avancé (rue de Theux 107), dedicated to progression and training.

## Who it's for

Curious people who want to try a sport without complicated gear, friends looking for an active after-work outing, regular climbers who want to progress. The gym is not open to under-12s: they head to Le Camp d'Approche instead. 12- to 16-year-olds come with an adult, or on their own with parental permission.

## Practical info

- **Opening hours**: Monday to Friday 9 am to 11 pm, weekends and public holidays 9 am to 9 pm.
- **Prices**: €14 per session (€12 reduced rate: ages 12-18, students and other conditions on the website), 10-session card €126, 4-week pass €50. Shoe rental: €3.50.
- **Before you go**: a member account is required, even for a first session; creating it online saves time on site.
- **Contact**: +32 2 343 43 21 (staffed 9 am to 4 pm).
