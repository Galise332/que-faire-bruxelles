When it's grey out and too early to head home, a bookshop makes a very good refuge: it's warm, nobody rushes you and you rarely leave empty-handed. Here are three addresses in central Brussels, each with its own character: a gilded room under the galleries, a large general bookshop and a place designed for children.

## 1. Tropismes

**Galerie des Princes 11, 1000 Bruxelles** · [tropismes.com](https://www.tropismes.com/)

Tucked inside the Galeries Royales Saint-Hubert, Tropismes has a setting you won't forget: gilded columns, mirrors and tables piled with books all the way to the back of the room. You'll find French and translated literature, crime fiction, poetry, fine arts, comics and a children's section sorted by age, from 0 to 13 and up. Open every day: Monday to Friday from 10 am to 6:30 pm, Saturday from 10:30 am to 7 pm and Sunday from 1:30 pm to 6:30 pm. Metro Gare Centrale or De Brouckère.

## 2. Filigranes

**Boulevard de Waterloo 25, 1000 Bruxelles** · [filigranes.be](https://www.filigranes.be/)

A well-known address among Brussels locals, in its new home on Boulevard de Waterloo since April. A large general bookshop where you move from novels to travel guides, from art books to essays, with new releases and the team's favourites front and centre. Open 7 days a week: Monday to Friday from 10:30 am to 6:30 pm, Saturday from 10:30 am to 7 pm and Sunday from 10:30 am to 6 pm. Closed on public holidays. Metro Louise or Porte de Namur.

## 3. Le Wolf

**Rue de la Violette 20, 1000 Bruxelles** · [lewolf.be](https://www.lewolf.be/)

A stone's throw from the Grand-Place, Le Wolf is a place entirely devoted to children and children's literature. It has a bookshop, exhibitions by illustrators, a pram-friendly café for the little ones, plus workshops and holiday courses. The exhibitions can be visited Tuesday to Saturday from 10 am to 5 pm. The ideal address for an afternoon with kids, who'll leave with a story to read at bedtime. Metro Gare Centrale.
