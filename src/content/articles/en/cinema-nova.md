Walking into the Nova for the first time, many people wonder whether the place is still under construction. That's intentional: peeling walls, exposed brick, traces of a century of history left in plain sight. At 3 rue d'Arenberg, the building has been a café, a cabaret, a theatre, then a Pathé cinema from 1908 and the Studio Arenberg from 1936 to 1987. Abandoned and turned into a storeroom for old furniture, the screen was rediscovered in 1996 by a group of film lovers. The Nova opened in 1997 as an "emergency cinema": its name comes from the stars that shine very brightly for a very short time.

## What to do there

You come to see independent films that slip through the cracks of mainstream distribution. The programme changes every two months and is organised into themed modules chosen collectively by the team. Alongside it, regular events come back again and again: the Open Screen, an open stage for anyone's films; the Live Soundtracks, where musicians create the soundtrack of silent films live; the Nocturnes, devoted to cult films and genre cinema; and Cineketje, screenings for young and old alike.

The Nova isn't just about the screen. In the auditorium and in the basement bar-foyer, you'll come across exhibitions, debates, workshops, performances, concerts and the Microboutiek, always linked to the image or the theme of the moment.

## Who it's for

Curious minds who want to see what you can't find anywhere else, lovers of places with a soul, families thanks to Cineketje, and those on a tight budget: several events are free. The auditorium is accessible to people with reduced mobility (a ramp can be requested for the step from the pavement), but the bar and toilets are not.

## Practical info

- **Screenings**: according to the programme, available on the website.
- **Prices**: "regular" screening €7 (full price), €4 (reduced: students, unemployed, pensioners, undocumented people) or €10 (support). Short screening (under 60 minutes): €4. Exhibitions, Open Screen and Prima Nova: free. 5-screening pass: €30, or €15 with a reduction. Article 27 and Cineville tickets accepted.
- **Booking**: no booking or advance sales unless stated otherwise. Just turn up.
- **Getting there**: rue d'Arenberg 3, next to the Galeries royales Saint-Hubert.
- **Contact**: 02 511 24 77.
