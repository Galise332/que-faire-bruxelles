Fancy a cheesecake made with dried porcini? At Café des Spores, on Chaussée d'Alsemberg in Saint-Gilles, mushrooms turn up on every plate, from starter to dessert. They are the thread running through the meal, course after course.

## What's on the menu

The cooking follows the seasons: the kitchen only works with fresh produce, and the menu changes with the season and what's available. Fish, meat and vegetarian options all have their place.

The menu on the website at the time of writing gives a sense of the style. To start, a beetroot carpaccio with ricotta and yuzu shimeji. For the main course, white miso salmon with roast portobello, rosemary-smoked duck fillet with porcini sauce, or house-made gnocchi in a truffle cream. For dessert, that cheesecake, with salted butter caramel and hazelnuts.

The bread, 100% natural sourdough and certified organic, is made next door in the restaurant's own bakery, Hopla Geiss Bakery. And for anyone who isn't much of a mushroom person, a mushroom-free option is always available.

## Who it's for

Curious diners who love characterful produce and want to taste something different over dinner. Mixed tables too, since nobody has to eat mushrooms. The restaurant only opens in the evening.

For more than 8 people, there is the "Groups and private hire" offer: the venue has three spaces, including a wine cellar and an upstairs room.
