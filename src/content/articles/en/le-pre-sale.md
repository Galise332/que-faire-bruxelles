On Rue de Flandre, a few steps from Place Sainte-Catherine, Le Pré Salé has been serving mussels for more than 40 years, at the same address. The neighbourhood has changed a lot around it, but the house has kept its course: simple, generous Belgian cooking, served without fuss in a white-tiled room where a large stained-glass window crowns the open kitchen.

## What's on the plate

Mussels are the house speciality. They are cooked to order and arrive in the pot with fries, in seven preparations that stay on the menu all year: marinière, white wine, garlic, cream, curry, garlic and cream, and roquefort. Enough to come back several times without eating the same thing twice.

For those who aren't "mussels" people, the menu covers the Belgian classics: grey shrimp croquettes, cheese croquettes, carbonnade flamande, stoemp, meatballs in tomato sauce, fish and homemade desserts. The full menu, with prices, is published on the restaurant's website; the prices displayed in the dining room prevail.

## Who it's for

For giving someone new to Brussels a taste of moules-frites, for a meal with family or friends, or for lunch after a stroll between Sainte-Catherine and Saint-Géry. The house describes itself as a place where regulars from the centre, families, groups of friends and passing visitors all meet. Groups are welcome on request: the restaurant replies with a proposal depending on availability.

## Practical info

- **Opening hours**: open every day, Monday to Sunday, from 12pm to 3pm and from 6pm to 10:30pm.
- **Mussels at lunchtime**: yes, they are served from the lunch service, just as in the evening.
- **Booking**: recommended, especially at weekends and in the evening, online on the website or by phone on +32 2 513 65 45.
- **Groups**: request via the Groups page of the website or by phone.
- **Getting there**: in the pedestrian zone of the Sainte-Catherine district, on foot from Place Sainte-Catherine and Place Saint-Géry.
