Some places you walk past without a second thought, and then there is Le Cirio. In the heart of the Bourse district, this brasserie has been welcoming people from Brussels since 1886. The Art Nouveau interior wasn't recreated for tourists: it has simply always been there, and that's exactly what makes it so charming.

## What to do there

Drop in for a mid-morning coffee, a quiet meal or a break at the end of the day after a stroll around the centre. Le Cirio describes itself as a traditional Belgian brasserie: the menu highlights typically Belgian dishes such as carbonnade (Flemish beef stew). The atmosphere is friendly and unfussy, and the place has kept the slow rhythm of old cafés where you can linger at your table for a long time.

On sunny days there is also a terrace. For groups, the house offers booking and private-hire options, listed on its website.

## Who it's for

For visitors who want to see a period Brussels interior without going to a museum, for architecture lovers, and for anyone looking for a table near the Grand-Place away from the bustle of the tourist terraces. The place describes itself as ideal for families, so you can come with children.

## Practical info

- **Opening hours**: every day, 10am to midnight.
- **Address**: Rue de la Bourse 18, 1000 Bruxelles.
- **Booking**: online on the website or by phone on +32 2 512 13 95.
- **Payment**: debit card, Visa, Mastercard, Payconiq or cash.
- **Getting there**: Bourse – Grand-Place premetro station, a few steps away.
