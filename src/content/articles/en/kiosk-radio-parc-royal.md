In the middle of the paths of Parc Royal, there is a small wooden kiosk with music coming out of it. This is no passing sound system: it is a real radio studio. Since 2017, Kiosk Radio has been broadcasting from here non-stop, 24 hours a day, and a tiny bar attached to the studio lets you stop for a moment and listen live to whatever is on air.

## What to do there

You settle in the park, coffee in hand, while a DJ or a guest plays a few metres away. The programming doesn't lock itself into any one style: from jazz to experimental music, from ambient to hip-hop, from rock to electronic. Live sets, recorded shows and conversations with artists alternate throughout the day, with particular attention to scenes that live outside the major circuits.

The bar serves coffee, local beers, natural wines and organic sodas every day. Kiosk Radio is a non-profit community radio: what is sold at the counter goes directly towards keeping the station running. The station also organises parties and projects outside the kiosk, including its annual festival Woodblocks, and it is a music partner of KANAL – Centre Pompidou.

Not on site? The radio can be heard anywhere: on the website, on the iOS app, on Sonos speakers, or as a replay on SoundCloud, Mixcloud and YouTube.

## Who it's for

Music lovers who want to discover something other than ordinary radio, walkers in Parc Royal looking for a break, visitors who want to see a corner of Brussels you won't find in the standard guidebooks. The setting, an open-air park, also makes for a lovely family stroll in the afternoon.

## Practical information

- **Bar opening hours**: every day, from noon to 10pm.
- **Booking**: not possible, the kiosk works on a first come, first served basis. No advance drink sales and no tokens.
- **Where**: in the Parc de Bruxelles (Parc Royal), in the heart of the city.
- **Metro**: Parc (lines 1 and 5), a stone's throw away.
- **Listen**: live on kioskradio.com, 24/7.
