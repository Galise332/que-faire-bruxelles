Next to the Grand-Place, on Rue des Pierres, a 100-seat room has been living to the rhythm of jazz since 2000. The Music Village has hosted more than 6,000 concerts since it opened, and guests from over 105 countries. The formula is simple: a stage, an audience close to the musicians, and a concert almost every night.

## What you do there

You come to hear live music, six to seven days a week all year round, with over 300 concerts a year. Jazz is at the heart of it, in all its colours (fusion, Afro, Brazilian, Cuban, manouche), alongside blues, rhythm & blues, New Orleans and funk. An evening usually runs in two 45-minute sets.

The club also backs the next generation. On Wednesdays, except in July and August, it gives carte blanche to a rising talent from the Belgian and international scene. On Tuesdays, and also on Wednesdays in July and August, young musicians gather for a jam session with free entry.

You can have dinner on site, à la carte, from opening time and during the first set. Eating is not compulsory, and the bar also serves soft drinks and teas.

## Who it's for

The club is as much for connoisseurs as for people discovering jazz. It works for a night out for two, with friends, or with a visitor passing through. Under-18s are welcome with their parents. Gift vouchers also let you give the evening as a present.

## Practical info

- **Hours**: doors at 7:30 pm, concert at 8:30 pm, ending around 11 pm.
- **Price**: €18 to €25 depending on the programme, paid at the door on the day of the concert (cards accepted). Jam sessions and the "Singers showcase" are free.
- **Booking**: preferably through the form on each concert's page.
- **Membership card**: €15 for 12 months, valid for two people, with €2 off most concerts.
- **Getting there**: De Brouckère metro (100 m), Interparking Grand-Place car park.
