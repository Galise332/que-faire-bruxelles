A table, a box you've never opened and a few friends: that's all it takes to fill an evening. In Brussels, several places open their shelves to people who want to play without buying the game. Here are three very different addresses: two board game bars and a non-profit club that meets every Sunday.

## 1. Chez Cubitus

**Boulevard Général Jacques 253, 1050 Ixelles** and **Avenue Georges Henri 319, 1200 Woluwe-Saint-Lambert** · [chezcubitus.be](https://chezcubitus.be/)

Two addresses, one idea: a €5 fee gets you access to the games. It's also a shop, so you can leave with the game you've just discovered. The menu has Belgian soft drinks, beers from local breweries and light meals. The collections vary slightly between the two bars, so if you have your heart set on a particular game, call ahead. Coming alone or in a small group? Open tables let you play with people you've never met: every Wednesday in Woluwe and on the first Tuesday of the month in Ixelles, where chess players also meet on Mondays at 7pm. Ixelles is open Monday to Thursday from 5pm to midnight, Friday from 5pm to 1am, Saturday from 2pm to 1am and Sunday from 2pm to 8pm. Woluwe keeps the same hours but closes on Mondays. Book on the website.

## 2. Le Bar à Jeux

**Chaussée d'Alsemberg 136, 1060 Saint-Gilles** · [lebarajeux.shop](https://lebarajeux.shop/)

For €5 per person you get access to a library of around 500 games, from classics to new releases, and the team will guide you and help you choose. There's a game shop on site too. The bar is open Tuesday to Friday from 4pm to midnight, Saturday from 2pm to midnight and Sunday from 2pm to 10pm; it's closed on Mondays. To book, call +32 2 850 23 83 during opening hours.

## 3. Alpa Ludismes

**Rue Charles Malis 71, 1080 Molenbeek** · [alpaludismes.net](http://www.alpaludismes.net/)

No bar here: this is a non-profit club, based at the Club Arc-en-ciel and open every Sunday from 2pm to 10pm, including during the holidays. Its library holds nearly a thousand games, from family favourites to titles for serious players. It works simply: you suggest a game to whoever is free and start with the volunteers. Your first visit is free, then a day costs €3 (€2 for teenagers). There's also an annual membership at €36 (€24 for teenagers). The club welcomes adults and teenagers from age 12, provided they have some experience of board games. Take the metro to Beekkant or Gare de l'Ouest, or tram 82 to the Mennekens stop.
